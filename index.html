Engineered for Speed, Clarity & Smoothness

Built from scratch in pure Java without bloated support libraries—keeping the signed universal APK under 410 KB while delivering flagship Pixel responsiveness.

🚀

Continuous Pixel App Morph

LaunchTransitionView morphs from exact icon bounds into rounded Material You splash surfaces with separated adaptive foreground/background parallax and warm/cold pre-warming.

⚡

Auto 60–180 Hz Display Pacing

DisplayRefreshHelper detects panel refresh modes (60, 90, 120, up to 180 Hz) and scales fling physics, overshoot tension, and Surface.setFrameRate on interaction.

❄

Adaptive Android 11–17 Blur

Uses native RenderEffect & SurfaceFlinger wallpaper blur on Android 12–17, and automatically drops to a zero-GPU-cost solid scrim on Android 11 so motion stays silky at 120 Hz.

🔍

Full-Color Search Capsule

Authentic 4-color Google G, Mic, and Lens shortcuts paired with inline app ranking, live web suggestions, Play Store search fallback, and deterministic 12dp swipe-vs-tap isolation.

🌦

Standalone At a Glance Weather

Works on Pixel and non-Pixel phones alike using Open-Meteo and real-time NetworkCallback sync across Wi-Fi and mobile data without requiring Google Smartspace privileges.

🗂

DragLayer, Folders & Theming

Multi-page chip drag overlay, top Remove & Uninstall drop zones, smart auto-dissolving folders, and dynamic Light/Dark Material You palette synchronization.
